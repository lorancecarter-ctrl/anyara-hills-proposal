03 — The Land

One acre. Freehold.
Individual title.

428 lots across 584 acres, of which Phase 1 comprises 238 lots. Freehold agricultural land held under individual title — the rarest form of ownership at this scale near Kuala Lumpur.

What you may build

Under the National Land Code, an agricultural title permits building on up to 20% of the land footprint.

43,560 sf×20%=8,712 sf

Buildable footprint on a standard one-acre lot.

  • Limited to one acre per individual name, or held in a company name
  • Title may not be converted from Agricultural to Residential, as stated in the SPA
  • Quit rent and assessment on agricultural land are substantially lower than residential
