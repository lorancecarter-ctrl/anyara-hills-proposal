Upload the price list

An Excel (.xlsx, .xls) or CSV file. The first row must be the column headings. Lot numbers already in the system are updated; new ones are added. Nothing is written until you review the summary and confirm.

ColumnRequiredNotes
Lot NoYesUnique. Also accepts Lot, Lot Number.
Land Size (sf)YesSquare feet. Also Size, SF, Land Size.
List PriceYesRinggit. Also Price, List Price (RM).
SPA PriceEitherThe nett price. Privilege is worked out as list − SPA.
PrivilegeEitherEntitlement/discount, if your sheet states it directly instead of an SPA price.
StatusNoAvailable, Reserved, Booked or Sold. Defaults to Available.
PhaseNoFree text.
CategoryNoSignature, Premium, Private — free text.
NotesNoFree text.

Nett price and price psf are calculated by the database — don't include them. Download a template.
